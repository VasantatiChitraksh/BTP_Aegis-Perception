09 / WHAT THE COMPARISON CAN SUPPORT

Attention Pix2Pix and LiteWeather v2 started randomly. Restormer and RAMiT used deraining weights; TransWeather used
All-Weather weights; PromptIR used all-in-one weights. Unequal pretraining means this is a system comparison, not an isolated
architecture test. Pretraining overlap is not ruled out, particularly for RESIDE fog. Single-seed ranks and small score gaps do not
establish statistical superiority. Cluster-bootstrap intervals are retained in the metrics JSON; they do not measure training-seed
uncertainty.




Local handoff: artifacts/models/<model>/ contains best.pt, inference.pt, metrics, history and SHA256SUMS. Original run folders and earlier
Restormer results remain preserved.
