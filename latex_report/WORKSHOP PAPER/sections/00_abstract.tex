\begin{abstract}
A restorer for edge vision must improve image fidelity while preserving task accuracy and fitting the target device. We evaluate six systems on 2,334 held-out rain, fog, snow, and smoke pairs. Restormer leads at 32.5831\,dB balanced PSNR and 0.929690 SSIM. The best of eight continuation arms gains 0.0288\,dB over matched continued training, and validation retains the original checkpoint. In an earlier local 72-image DAWN pilot, a $256\times448$ restoration path raises pooled AP$_{50:95}$ from 29.97\% to 32.86\%. In the labelled Nano evaluation, the $128\times128$ cascade reduces AP$_{50:95}$ from 29.59\% to 14.50\% and runs at 0.61--0.62 frames/s in DAWN replay. Matched controls show a truck missed after resizing alone, alongside a native-resolution rain recovery and smoke regression. The 8-bit weight-grid screen fails output agreement on fog and smoke.
\end{abstract}
