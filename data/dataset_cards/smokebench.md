# SmokeBench

- **ID:** `smokebench`
- **Role:** paired real-smoke restoration/generalization benchmark
- **Contents:** 9,875 train pairs and 100 test pairs
- **Domain:** surveillance imagery; not specifically road driving
- **Official project:** <https://github.com/ncfjd/SmokeBench>
- **Paper:** <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12701>
- **Local archive:** `data/archives/smokebench.zip`
- **SHA-256:** `0fa5f81e5c1fe7308cab21f14556653db36f7afbd8ed607d57632fc9154c22f0`
- **Rights:** verify the dataset release terms before redistribution

SmokeBench adds a modern, substantially larger paired source instead of
overfitting the collection to the small historical archive. Its surveillance
domain is useful for smoke appearance diversity but cannot by itself establish
ADAS performance.
