# O-HAZE

- **ID:** `o_haze`
- **Role:** real paired outdoor dehazing benchmark
- **Scale:** 45 outdoor scenes in the challenge dataset
- **Official source:** <https://data.vision.ee.ethz.ch/cvl/ntire18/o-haze/>
- **Local archive:** `data/archives/o_haze.zip`
- **SHA-256:** `538421bb80798da2298a9b4f0478ca59ff37e5a305ba57a8a9a8e03ec477be09`
- **Rights:** official page provides citation/download; verify redistribution terms

O-HAZE supplies real haze and corresponding haze-free imagery, but it is small
and not road-specific. Keep the official split/protocol and do not infer
detector improvement from restoration metrics alone.
